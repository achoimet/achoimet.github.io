How it works

  1. 1

    Your phone locks on training days

    Open any other app and LiftOnly brings you straight back to today's workout.

  2. 2

    Check every set

    Tap a set when it's done. Did fewer reps? Hold it and log what you really did.

  3. 3

    Finish, and it's yours again

    The last set unlocks your phone for the rest of the day. Every set is saved.
