How it works

  1. 1

    Your phone locks on training days

    Open any other app and LiftOnly brings you straight back to today's workout.

  2. 2

    Check every set

    Tap a set when it's done. Did fewer reps? Hold it and log what you really did.

  3. 3

    Finish, and it's yours again

    The last set unlocks your phone for the rest of the day. Every set is saved.

Built for the gym floor

Your program, your way

Starts with a push / pull / legs week. Change days, exercises, sets, reps and loads anytime.

Fix the weight mid-workout

Plate too heavy? − / + right on the lock screen. Done sets keep the weight they were done with.

Every set saved

Weight, reps and the rest before each set, to see the weight you lift per muscle, week over week.

Train on a rest day

Feel like going anyway? "Train now" locks you in until it's done.

Pick the main muscle

Disagree on what a squat or a dip trains? Choose the main muscle per exercise.

Works with the screen off

Put the phone down between sets. The rest clock sits in a notification and still alerts you.

Never trapped

  • Phone calls and emergency calls always go through
  • Settings stay open, so you can switch the lock off anytime
  • Real emergency at home? Hold Skip today for 10 seconds

Private by design

  • No account, no ads, no tracking
  • Your workouts stay on your phone
  • The lock only sees which app is opening. It can't read your screen
